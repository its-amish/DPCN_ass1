Question–Question (Item–Item) Correlation Network

Constructed by evaluating pairwise Spearman rank correlations (ρ > 0.35) across 60 Likert statements from 91 students. Identifies ideological anchors (Eigenvector Centrality), cross-domain bridges (Betweenness Centrality), and modular belief communities (Louvain algorithm, assortativity r = 0.3044).

Nodes sized by Eigenvector Centrality and colored by predefined domain: Blue (Tech), Orange (Education), Green (Society), Purple (Environment). Edge thickness reflects correlation weight (ρ > 0.35).

Eigenvector Centrality (X) vs. Betweenness Centrality (Y). Highlights key outlier bridge nodes such as S09 (diversity inclusion), S13 (digital literacy), and T12 (AI regulation).

Bipartite "Extremes" Network (τ ∈ {1, 5})

Filters exclusively for extreme convictions (Strongly Agree / Strongly Disagree), dropping moderate and neutral responses. Evaluates degree distributions, tests for scale-free vs. overdispersed topology, verifies Molloy–Reed percolation (κ = 59.0 >> 2), and analyzes the complete question projection (G_Q = K_60).

Student–Student Opinion Network

Builds a unipartite social projection of students using chance-adjusted agreement scores. Uncovers small-world characteristics, analyzes sparsified top-2 backbones, examines emphatic vs. typical student typologies, and performs domain-by-domain alignment comparisons.
